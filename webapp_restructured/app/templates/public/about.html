About the System

Dengue Prediction System

An AI-powered spatio-temporal prediction system for dengue fever cases in Yogyakarta Special Region, Indonesia.

This system leverages advanced machine learning techniques, specifically Spatio-Temporal Graph Neural Networks (STGNN), to predict dengue outbreaks by analyzing historical case data, climate variables, and environmental factors.

How It Works

  1. Data Collection: Historical dengue cases, climate data (temperature, humidity, rainfall), and NDVI satellite vegetation indices
  2. Spatial Analysis: Graph neural networks model relationships between different regencies based on proximity and environmental similarity
  3. Temporal Analysis: LSTM layers capture time-series patterns and seasonal trends in dengue transmission
  4. Prediction: The model generates monthly predictions with confidence intervals and risk classifications
  5. Recommendations: Automated health recommendations based on predicted risk levels to support decision-making

Data Sources

Dengue Cases

Monthly dengue case counts from health offices across 5 regencies in Yogyakarta

Climate Data

Temperature, humidity, precipitation, and other weather variables from NASA POWER and OpenWeather APIs

NDVI Data

Normalized Difference Vegetation Index from MODIS satellite imagery (NASA NEO)
