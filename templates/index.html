About the System

Intelligent Dengue Prediction

ExplainDengue utilizes cutting-edge machine learning technology to provide accurate and explainable dengue outbreak predictions. Our system integrates the Dengue Suspect Data from Early Warning Alert and Response System (EWARS) Indonesia that links with climatic impact drivers and environmental conditions from Open Weather Data and NASA POWER to health impacts in dengue cases across Regencies.

The Graph Neural Networks (GNN) architecture, enhanced with Gated Recurrent Units, captures spatial relationships and temporal patterns in dengue transmission, providing insights into the influencing factors across space and time.

Key Innovation

Our system contributes to improving the explainability of AI in infectious disease outbreak prediction, making complex predictions understandable for public health professionals.
